Baba described the three stages of Shaivite renunciation as:

Stage 1: Living in Shiva temple, meditating on the 5 Shiva Mantras, covered in ashes, loud laughter

Stage 2: Leave temple, wonder carrying khadaga, matted hair, public display of lewd behavior intended to gain people’s wrath (to gain their good karma)

Stage 3: Withdrawal to a remote place like a cremation ground, meditate upon the 5 sacred mantras and Om
