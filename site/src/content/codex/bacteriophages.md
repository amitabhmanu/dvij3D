**Bacteriophages** ("bacteria-eaters") are viruses that infect and destroy bacteria. In 1896 the British bacteriologist Ernest Hanbury Hankin reported that water from the Ganga and the Yamuna killed cholera bacteria. Two decades later Félix d'Herelle identified the agents responsible as viruses.

Dvij uses the idea to explain why bathing in the valley's river once healed people, and why it no longer does: something has killed the river's phages.
