The **Ashtadikpalas** are the eight guardians of the directions: Kubera (north), Yama (south), Indra (east), Varuna (west), and Ishana, Agni, Vayu and Nirrti at the corners. The first four are the more important *Mahadikpalas*. Add Brahma (zenith) and Vishnu (nadir) and there are ten.

Because each guards one direction, they are drawn on a 3×3 grid with the centre left empty. As Dvij, Bhavi and Kurup discover, that empty centre matters.
