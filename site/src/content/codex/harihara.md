**Harihara** is a combined form of Vishnu and Shiva: "Hari for Vishnu and Hara for Shiva." Sculptures show one half with Vishnu's crown and emblems and the other with Shiva's matted hair and trident, a statement that the two great gods are one.

Some of the earliest Harihara reliefs are in the 6th-century cave temples at Badami. In the valley, Kurup has seen a rock-cut Harihara near Vidgati's shrine on Mount Dronagiri.
