**Samkhya**, one of the oldest schools of Indian philosophy, describes two realities: *purusha* (pure consciousness) and *prakriti* (nature). In the round-nosed man's words, the energies latent in prakriti "manifest themselves in stages of evolution… Out of this process evolve the 24 principles." These principles, or **tattvas**, run from cosmic intelligence and ego to mind, the senses, the organs of action, and the subtle and gross elements.

The "Periodic Table of the Tattvas" below assigns each tattva a letter of the Devanagari alphabet.

**From the book's notes:**

| Mahabhutas | Earth | Water | Fire | Air | Ether |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  | क | ख | ग | घ | ङ |
| Tanmatas | Scent | Taste | Sight | Touch | Sound |
|  | च | छ | ज | झ | ञ |
| Karmendriyas | Excreting | Sexing | Grasping | Moving | Speaking |
|  | ट | ठ | ड | ढ | ण |
| Gyanendriyas | Smelling | Tasting | Seeing | Feeling | Hearing |
|  | त | थ | द | ध | न |
| Antahkarnas | Prakriti | Ahamkara | Buddhi | Manas | Purusha |
|  | प | फ | ब | भ | म |
| Kanchukas | Action | Knowledge | Desire | Time | Fate |
|  | य | र | ल | व | `B` |
| Shuddha-tattvas | Kriya | Gyana | Ichhna | Shakti | Shiva |
|  | श | ष | स | ह | क्ष |

The letters are the book's own. One cell, the kanchuka of *Fate*, is not recoverable: the manuscript sets this table in a legacy font, and that byte (`B`) falls outside the letters the rest of the table uses, so it is left as the source has it rather than guessed at.
