## In the book

"The myth says giants lived here." The vision behind Bhavi shows them: enormous men lifting boulders as though they weighed nothing and pitching them over the fort wall, more rocks already lying in heaps along the ramparts.

A few pages earlier the Professor had told a different giant story on the same theme — the **Vaastu Purusha**, "a giant man who once roamed the void and confronted the Gods", pinned face-down by all of them together and made to hold up the universe. Every building in the valley is laid out on his body.

## In tradition

Attributing very large old stonework to a vanished larger race is one of the oldest human explanations there is, and it survives wherever walls are built of blocks that look impossible to lift. What the book adds is the other Indian move: not a race of giants but a *single* giant, subdued and built on. The Vaastu Purusha mandala turns the defeated giant into a floor plan, and the gods who pinned him down each keep their square.

## And yet

The giants and the rakshasa share the page with an asteroid, and the asteroid gets the last panel. Bhavi offers both readings; Dvij asks how anyone could know; and the exchange ends on "Scientists deride ancient beliefs as myths…" — trailing off, unfinished, which is the book's honest answer.
