The **Ashtalakshmi** are eight forms of the goddess Lakshmi, each presiding over a different kind of fortune. Bhavi describes them as "ranging from most benevolent to most malevolent."

Their emblem, which Bhavi knows by heart, is the eight-pointed star formed by two overlapping squares, one turned 45°.
