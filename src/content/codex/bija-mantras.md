A *bija* ("seed") mantra is a one-syllable sound believed to hold special power. Each chakra's lotus has letters of the Devanagari alphabet on its petals, and one letter at its centre: its **bija akshara**. So a single letter is enough to identify a chakra, which Dvij and Bhavi turn to their advantage.
