The **Kaula** traditions follow *vamachara*, the "left-hand path" of tantra, which seeks liberation through the body rather than by denying it. Its rites use the **panchamakara**, the five "M"s that orthodox practice forbids: *madya* (wine), *mamsa* (meat), *matsya* (fish), *mudra* (parched grain, or gesture) and *maithuna* (union).

"Knowledge that hides in the darkness," the tantric baba calls it. Such practices "are secretly followed even by those who pretend to be most orthodox," but they "fail to reach elevated levels, for what they seek is pleasure and not knowledge."
