The **navagrahas** are the nine celestial influences of *jyotisha*: the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Mercury (Budha), Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and the lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu. Each is paired with one of the **navaratnas**, nine gems, and each presides over a direction.

At the valley's ground-breaking ceremony, the navaratnas are placed in the golden kalasha to appease the nine lords.

**From the book's notes:**

The association of Navagrahas with Navratnas – the fine gems- is well documented. But lesser so are the myriad characteristics of the Navagrahas that lend them an almost materialistic feel – almost as if one could reach out and touch these heavenly bodies that exert so much influence on most important part of our lives – the part that is yet to be lived.
